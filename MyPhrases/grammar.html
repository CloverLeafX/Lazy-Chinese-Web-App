Word Order Rule 1 of 10
时间 + 动词
Time + Verb
Subject → Time → Verb · Time word BEFORE the verb — always
In English: "I get up at 7" — verb first, time after. In Mandarin: time ALWAYS comes before the verb.
SUBJECTTIMEVERB
我wǒ 七qī点diǎn 起qǐ床chuáng
I7 o'clockget up
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ通tōng常cháng七qī点diǎn起qǐ床chuáng，就jiù算suàn周zhōu末mò也yě一yī样yàng。
I usually get up at 7, even on weekends.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ通tōng常cháng早zǎo上shang七qī点diǎn开kāi车chē去qù上shàng班bān。
I usually drive to work at 7am.
我wǒ昨zuó天tiān晚wǎn上shang十shí一yī点diǎn睡shuì觉jiào。
I went to sleep at 11pm last night.
⚠ Never: 我起床七点 ✗ — time cannot come after the verb
✓ Multiple time words: biggest → smallest → 我昨天早上七点起床
Word Order Rule 2 of 10
地点 + 动词
Location + Verb
Subject → 在+Place → Verb · 在+place BEFORE the verb
在 + place anchors WHERE the action happens — always before the verb, opposite to English.
SUBJECT在 + PLACEVERB
我wǒ 在zài家jiā 上shàng班bān
Iat homework
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ一yī周zhōu在zài家jiā上shàng班bān三sān天tiān。
I work from home three days a week.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ的de爱ài人rén不bù在zài悉xī尼ní上shàng班bān。
My partner doesn't work in Sydney.
她tā在zài餐cān厅tīng工gōng作zuò。
She works at a restaurant.
⚠ English: "works IN Sydney" — verb before location
✓ Negation: 不 goes before 在 → 不在悉尼上班
Word Order Rule 3 of 10
跟/和 + 人 + 动词
With Somebody + Verb
Subject → 跟/和+Person → Verb · 跟/和+person BEFORE the verb
跟 (with/to) and 和 (and/with) sit between subject and verb — never after like in English.
SUBJECT跟/和 + PERSONVERB
我wǒ 跟gēn朋péng友yǒu 吃chī饭fàn
Iwith friendseat
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ喜xǐ欢huān跟gēn朋péng友yǒu一yì起qǐ吃chī饭fàn喝hē啤pí酒jiǔ。
I like eating and drinking beer with friends.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ想xiǎng跟gēn家jiā人rén用yòng普pǔ通tōng话huà沟gōu通tōng。
I want to communicate with family in Mandarin.
我wǒ跟gēn我wǒ的de爱ài人rén一yī起qǐ去qù南nán非fēi。
I'm going to South Africa with my partner.
⚠ English: "eating WITH friends" — verb before "with"
✓ 跟 = with (doing together) | 和 = and/with (listing)
Word Order Rule 4 of 10
否定词位置
Negation Placement
Subject → 不/没 → Verb · Negation BEFORE the verb — always
不 (bù) = present/future/habitual · 没 (méi) = past negation or negating 有
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ不bù喜xǐ欢huān跑pǎo步bù。
I don't like running.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ没méi有yǒu去qù过guò中zhōng国guó。
I have never been to China.
我wǒ还hái没méi吃chī东dōng西xi。
I haven't eaten yet.
我wǒ今jīn天tiān不bù用yòng上shàng班bān。
I don't need to go to work today.
✓ 不用 = don't need to | 不能 = not allowed | 不会 = don't know how
✓ 不 negates present/future · 没 negates past
Word Order Rule 5 of 10
副词位置
Adverb Placement
Subject → 也/都/还/才/就 → Verb · Key adverbs BEFORE the verb
也/都/还/才/就 always slot between subject and verb — never after
ADVERBMEANINGEXAMPLE
也yěalso/too我wǒ也yě去qù
都dōuall/both我wǒ们men都dōu去qù
还háistill/also我wǒ还hái有yǒu
就jiùright away我wǒ就jiù来lái
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
就jiù算suàn上shàng班bān的de时shí候hou也yě学xué中zhōng文wén。
Even when at work I ALSO study Chinese.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ还hái没méi吃chī东dōng西xi。
I STILL haven't eaten yet.
⚠ Never: 我去也 ✗ — adverb always BEFORE the verb
Word Order Rule 6 of 10
双动词顺序
Double Verb Order
Subject → V1 (movement) → V2 (purpose) · Movement first, purpose second
When going somewhere TO DO something — movement verb first, purpose verb second.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ想xiǎng去qù中zhōng国guó旅lǚ游yóu。
I want to go to China to travel.
★ Your sentence
一yī到dào悉xī尼ní就jiù去qù吃chī火huǒ锅guō。
The moment I arrive in Sydney I'll go eat hotpot.
我wǒ们men去qù练liàn柔róu术shù。
We're going to train BJJ.
✓ Movement verbs: 去(go), 来(come), 回(return), 到(arrive)
Word Order Rule 7 of 10
量词
Measure Words
Number → 量词 → Noun · Always between number and noun
Every noun needs a measure word between the number and the noun — no exceptions. 个 (gè) is the safe default.
量词PINYINUSED FOREXAMPLE
个gègèpeople, general几jǐ个gè朋péng友yǒu
杯bēibēidrinks两liǎng杯bēi啤pí酒jiǔ
块kuàikuàipieces/flat一yī块kuài饼bǐng干gān
次cìcìtimes/occurrences三sān次cì
周zhōuzhōuweeks一yī周zhōu
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ一yī周zhōu打dǎ三sān次cì巴bā西xī柔róu术shù。
I do BJJ three times a week.
★ Your sentence 😄
我wǒ可kě以yǐ要yào一yī块kuài饼bǐng干gān吗ma？
Can I have a biscuit?
⚠ Never: 两朋友 ✗ — 个 is always required between 两 and 朋友
✓ 个 (gè) is the safe default for people and general objects
Word Order Rule 8 of 10
的的位置
的 Placement
Modifier + 的 → Noun · Descriptions come BEFORE the noun
的 connects a description to the noun — modifier always comes before the noun it describes.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ听tīng说shuō重chóng庆qìng有yǒu很hěn好hǎo吃chī的de火huǒ锅guō。
I heard Chongqing has really delicious hotpot.
★ Your sentence
番fān茄qié炒chǎo蛋dàn是shì我wǒ最zuì喜xǐ欢huān的de菜cài。
Tomato and egg is my favourite dish.
✓ Short adjectives (好, 大, 新) can skip 的 in casual speech: 好朋友
Word Order Rule 9 of 10
时长位置
Duration Placement
Verb → Duration · How long comes AFTER the verb
Duration (how long) comes after the verb — opposite to time words (which come before).
★ From your conversation
★ South Africa!
我wǒ们men会huì待dāi两liǎng个gè星xīng期qī。
We'll stay for two weeks.
★ Your sentence
我wǒ可kě以yǐ待dāi三sān十shí分fēn钟zhōng。
I can stay for thirty minutes.
他tā在zài悉xī尼ní住zhù了le十shí年nián。
He's lived in Sydney for ten years.
✓ Time WHEN = BEFORE verb: 七点起床
✓ Duration HOW LONG = AFTER verb: 学了三年
✓ If verb has object, repeat the verb: 我学普通话学了三年
Word Order Rule 10 of 10
话题-评论结构
Topic-Comment Structure
Topic, → Comment · State the topic first, then comment on it
Mandarin often front-loads the topic, then makes a statement about it. Very natural in spoken Mandarin.
TOPICCOMMENT
普pǔ通tōng话huà， 我wǒ不bù太tài会huì说shuō
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
普pǔ通tōng话huà，我wǒ不bù太tài会huì说shuō。
Mandarin, I can't really speak it well.
★ South Africa
南nán非fēi，我wǒ七qī月yuè要yào去qù。
South Africa, I'm going in July.
柔róu术shù，我wǒ练liàn了le很hěn多duō年nián了le。
BJJ, I've been training for many years.
✓ Very natural in spoken Mandarin — gives context before the main point
✓ English equivalent: "As for Mandarin, I can't really speak it"
Grammar Pattern 1 of 12
是...的
Emphasising HOW / WHEN / WHERE
S + 是 + circumstance + V + 的 · Spotlight on context
Use 是...的 to highlight HOW, WHEN or WHERE something happened — not what happened.
SUBJECT是CIRCUMSTANCE + VERB的
你nǐ 是shì 怎zěn么me学xué普pǔ通tōng话huà 的de？
★ From your conversation
★ Your vocab
我wǒ是shì一yī个gè人rén住zhù的de。
I live alone. (emphasising BY MYSELF)
你nǐ是shì怎zěn么me学xué普pǔ通tōng话huà的de？
How did you learn Mandarin? (emphasising METHOD)
他tā是shì去qù年nián来lái的de。
He came last year. (emphasising WHEN)
✓ 是 can be dropped in casual speech: 你怎么来的？
✓ Past tense only — don't use for future events
Grammar Pattern 2 of 12
把
Moving the object before the verb
S + 把 + OBJECT + V + result · What happens TO the object
把 takes the object and places it BEFORE the verb — focuses on the result. Must have completion after verb.
SUBJECT把OBJECTVERB + RESULT
我wǒ 把bǎ 东dōng西xi 收shōu起qǐ来lái
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
把bǎ东dōng西xi收shōu起qǐ来lái。
Put the things away.
他tā把bǎ啤pí酒jiǔ喝hē完wán了le。
He finished all the beer. 😄
请qǐng把bǎ手shǒu机jī放fàng下xià。
Please put your phone down.
⚠ 我把饭做 ✗ — must have completion: 我把饭做好了 ✓
✓ Common results: 起来(put away), 好(done), 完(finished), 掉(get rid of)
Grammar Pattern 3 of 12
被
Passive voice
S + 被 + doer + V · Something was done TO the subject
被 marks passive — the subject receives the action. Often carries a negative/undesirable tone.
★ From your conversation
★ Your vocab
我wǒ被bèi老lǎo板bǎn表biǎo扬yáng了le。
I was praised by my boss.
我wǒ的de手shǒu机jī被bèi偷tōu了le。
My phone was stolen.
他tā被bèi说shuō服fú了le。
He was convinced.
✓ 被 often carries a negative/undesirable tone
✓ Doer can be omitted: 我被骗了 = I was cheated (by someone)
Grammar Pattern 4 of 12
越...越...
The more...the more...
越 + condition + 越 + result · Two qualities that grow together
As one thing increases, the other follows. 越来越 = one thing growing. 越A越B = two things linked.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
我wǒ觉jué得de中zhōng文wén越yuè来lái越yuè难nán了le。
I feel Chinese is getting harder and harder.
越yuè练liàn越yuè好hǎo。
The more you train, the better you get. (BJJ 😄)
越yuè说shuō越yuè流liú利lì。
The more you speak, the more fluent you get.
越yuè吃chī越yuè想xiǎng吃chī。
The more you eat, the more you want to eat.
✓ 越来越 = one thing growing: more and more [adj]
✓ 越A越B = two things linked: the more A, the more B
Grammar Pattern 5 of 12
比
Comparing two things
A + 比 + B + adjective · A is more [adj] than B
比 sits between two things — plain adjective at the end. No 更 or special comparative form needed.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
悉xī尼ní比bǐ伦lún敦dūn热rè。
Sydney is hotter than London.
★ Your sentence 😄
柔róu术shù比bǐ跑pǎo步bù有yǒu趣qù。
BJJ is more interesting than running.
今jīn天tiān比bǐ昨zuó天tiān冷lěng一yī点diǎn。
Today is a little colder than yesterday.
⚠ English: "hotter THAN" — comparative adjective + than
✓ Mandarin: 比 + plain adjective — no 更 or special form needed
✓ Add degree: 比...热一点 (a bit more) / 比...热多了 (much more)
Grammar Pattern 6 of 12
没有
Negative comparison — not as...as
A + 没有 + B + adj · A is NOT as [adj] as B
没有 flips the comparison — A falls short of B. Same adjective used as with 比.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence 😄
跑pǎo步bù没méi有yǒu柔róu术shù有yǒu趣qù。
Running is not as interesting as BJJ.
今jīn天tiān没méi有yǒu昨zuó天tiān冷lěng。
Today is not as cold as yesterday.
悉xī尼ní没méi有yǒu香xiāng港gǎng热rè闹nào。
Sydney is not as lively as Hong Kong.
✓ 比 = A is MORE than B → 柔术比跑步有趣
✓ 没有 = A is NOT AS much as B → 跑步没有柔术有趣
Grammar Pattern 7 of 12
V + 得
How well something is done
VERB + 得 + description · Quality/degree of the action
得 sandwiches between a verb and how well it's performed. To negate: put 不 after 得.
★ From your conversation
★ Your actual sentence!
你nǐ的de公gōng司sī去qù年nián做zuò得de不bù错cuò。
Your company did pretty well last year.
★ Your vocab
我wǒ普pǔ通tōng话huà说shuō得de不bù太tài好hǎo。
I don't speak Mandarin that well.
你nǐ说shuō得de很hěn好hǎo！
You speak really well!
他tā柔róu术shù练liàn得de很hěn好hǎo。
He trains BJJ really well.
✓ To negate: put 不 after 得 → 做得不好 / 说得不清楚
✓ If verb has object, repeat the verb: 做饭做得好 (not 做饭得好)
Grammar Pattern 8 of 12
V + 不了/得了
Can or cannot complete an action
VERB + 不了 (cannot) / 得了 (can) · Physical possibility
Expresses whether completing an action is physically/logistically possible — not willingness or permission.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
今jīn天tiān去qù不bù了liǎo。
Can't go today. (impossible, not just unwilling)
★ Your sentence
这zhè个ge我wǒ吃chī不bù了liǎo。
I can't finish this / too much for me.
这zhè件jiàn事shì我wǒ一yī个gè人rén做zuò不bù了liǎo。
I can't do this alone.
✓ 不了 = physically/logistically impossible
✓ 不能 = not allowed | 不会 = don't know how | 不了 = can't complete
Grammar Pattern 9 of 12
先...然后...
First...then...
先 + V1 + 然后 + V2 · Deliberate sequence of actions
先 = first, 然后 = then — sequences steps in deliberate order. Gap between actions is fine.
★ From your conversation
★ Your vocab 😄
先xiān练liàn柔róu术shù，然rán后hòu回huí家jiā吃chī饭fàn。
First train BJJ, then go home and eat.
★ Your sentence 😄
先xiān刷shuā牙yá，然rán后hòu睡shuì觉jiào。
First brush teeth, then sleep.
先xiān试shì试shi，然rán后hòu再zài决jué定dìng。
Try it first, then decide.
✓ 先 alone = go first: 我先来！← you used this in your group call!
✓ 然后 can start a new sentence: 先吃。然后去。
Grammar Pattern 10 of 12
一...就...
As soon as / the moment
一 + V1 + 就 + V2 · Immediate — zero gap between actions
As soon as the first thing happens, the second follows immediately. No gap.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
一yī回huí家jiā就jiù吃chī饭fàn。
As soon as I get home I eat.
★ Your sentence
一yī到dào悉xī尼ní就jiù去qù吃chī火huǒ锅guō。
The moment I arrive in Sydney I'll eat hotpot.
一yī下xià班bān就jiù去qù练liàn柔róu术shù。
As soon as work finishes I go train BJJ.
一yī起qǐ床chuáng就jiù看kàn手shǒu机jī。
As soon as I get up I look at my phone.
✓ 一...就 = immediacy — zero gap between actions
✓ 先...然后 = deliberate sequence — gap between actions is fine
Grammar Pattern 11 of 12
的时候
When / while doing something
Action + 的时候 + main clause · Sets the time frame
的时候 attaches to an ACTION to mean "when/while doing that action". Tense carried by the main clause.
★ From your conversation — used this many times today!
★ Your sentence
就jiù算suàn上shàng班bān的de时shí候hou也yě学xué中zhōng文wén。
Even when at work I study Chinese.
★ Your sentence 😄
跑pǎo步bù的de时shí候hou很hěn无wú聊liáo。
When running it's boring.
★ Your sentence
小xiǎo的de时shí候hou，我wǒ妈mā妈ma让ràng我wǒ们men去qù中zhōng文wén学xué校xiào。
When I was young, mum made us go to Chinese school.
★ Your joke 😄
有yǒu钱qián的de时shí候hou，我wǒ想xiǎng去qù中zhōng国guó旅lǚ游yóu。
When I have money, I want to travel to China.
✓ 的时候 attaches to an ACTION — not a static state
✓ Tense carried by the MAIN clause — 的时候 itself is tense-neutral
Grammar Pattern 12 of 12
如果...就...
If...then...
如果 + condition + 就 + result · Conditional sentences
如果 sets the condition — 就 delivers the result. 就 can be dropped in casual speech.
★ From your conversation
★ Your sentence
如rú果guǒ你nǐ每měi天tiān学xué一yī点diǎn中zhōng文wén，我wǒ觉jué得de你nǐ会huì进jìn步bù。
If you study a little Chinese every day, I think you'll improve.
★ Your joke 😄
如rú果guǒ我wǒ有yǒu钱qián，我wǒ就jiù去qù中zhōng国guó旅lǚ游yóu。
If I have money, I'll travel to China.
如rú果guǒ你nǐ练liàn得de多duō，就jiù会huì越yuè来lái越yuè好hǎo。
If you train more, you'll keep getting better. (GP7 + GP4!)
如rú果guǒ不bù学xué，就jiù不bù会huì进jìn步bù。
If you don't study, you won't improve.
✓ 就 can be dropped in casual speech: 如果有时间，来我家
✓ 要是 (yào shì) is a casual alternative for 如果
✓ Add 的话 at end for softness: 如果有时间的话
